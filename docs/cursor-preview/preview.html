Cursor redraw

Three minimal SVG directions for the default pointer, shown on light and dark backgrounds. Slate keeps the familiar Aero silhouette with a cleaner outline; Feather is slimmer; Soft is slightly rounder and calmer. Choose one and it will be rendered as a vector at the exact on-screen pixel size, so it stays sharp at 100–500% mouse scale.
